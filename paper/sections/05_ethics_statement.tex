\section*{Ethics Statement}
The authors have read and follow the ethical requirements for publication in \emph{Data in
Brief}. The work involves human participants as contributors of messages they received, and part
of the data was taken from social media. \textbf{Community batch.} \FILL{N\textsubscript{c}}
messages were gathered before the protocol through a Google Form that stated the research purpose, promised anonymisation, said the
result would be contributed free to the research community and that submitting meant agreeing
to academic use, but did not state permanent publication or offer withdrawal (text filed
verbatim in the repository); direct requests, and public Facebook and Threads posts in which people showed scam
SMS they had received. Only the scam text was taken from a post, by hand, never by crawler; no
name, handle, post link or image was retained, sender numbers were reduced to a country prefix,
and screenshots were deleted once transcribed. The texts are messages broadcast by scammers to
strangers, in which neither the poster nor the sender holds a privacy interest. Consent for this
batch was not obtained before collection and cannot be obtained afterwards; \FILL{the ethics
application asked explicitly whether it may be used, and the answer was …}. \textbf{Protocol
corpus.}
\FILL{Ethics approval was granted by (approving body), reference (number), on (date). / The
institution's written answer that no review process covers this study is filed in the public
repository in place of an approval.} Informed consent was obtained from every contributor
before collection. The collection protocol and the consent form were published and timestamped
in the public repository before the first message was collected, so the order of events can be
verified independently of the authors. Participation was anonymous, voluntary, unconnected to
any assessment, and recruited by a person with no assessment role over the contributors;
withdrawal needed no reason and was possible until deposit.

No personal data was collected. On the primary route, redaction ran on the contributor's
device and raw text never reached the researchers. On the screenshot route, images were
stripped of metadata on receipt, transcribed, checked, redacted and deleted before publication.
The contributor identifier is withheld from the published files. No message written by a
person was collected, so no third party's words appear without their consent. The pilot batch
from the lead author's own inbox, used to exercise the pipeline, is \FILL{included / excluded}
from the corpus, as stated in the ethics application. Collection complies with Decree
13/2023/ND-CP on personal data protection~\cite{decree13_sms} and Vietnam's Personal Data
Protection Law (effective 2026-01-01). No content was crawled from social media platforms.

The external benchmark is a published dataset reused under its CC BY 4.0 licence with
attribution; its labels are shipped unchanged and no additional data about any person was
derived from it. No content was crawled from any platform; the social-media posts above were
read and copied by hand.
