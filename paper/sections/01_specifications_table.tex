Data collection & \emph{Community batch (pre-protocol):} scam SMS gathered through a Google Form,
direct requests, and hand-copied public Facebook and Threads posts, with nothing retained about any
contributor or poster; redacted and ingested by the same pipeline. \emph{Protocol corpus:} volunteers paste messages into an offline
redaction page on their own phone and submit the redacted text, or send cropped, blacked-out
screenshots that are OCR-transcribed, hand-checked, redacted and then deleted. Only machine-sent
messages (brandname or shortcode senders); never person-to-person. Two independent human
annotators per message; adjudication of disagreements. Templates by word 4-shingle Jaccard
clustering on a normalized view; the split assigned per template. \emph{External benchmark:}
the Quality-Assured Vietnamese SMS Phishing Dataset~\cite{qavn_sms} (CC BY 4.0), placeholder
tokens remapped, rows failing the redaction rule dropped and counted, labels kept as published,
held out entirely. \\
